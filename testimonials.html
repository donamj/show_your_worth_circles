What Our Community Says

Discover how Show Your Worth Circles have empowered women to build a success mindset.

A glimpse from our first cohort wrap-up in Dallas — Show Your Worth Leaders Meetup Dallas. A vibrant community of women gathering to share their transformative experiences.

AB

Apurva Bhagali

Global PM at Microsoft

"Show your worth was my entry into kick starting my dead reading goals, so I had two wins when I finished this book! 10 sessions with an amazing cohort gave me the 2 hours of FOCUSED PROFESSIONAL SELF-CARE I didn't even know I was looking for."

"A dedicated time to bring out my leadership qualities, flaws, expectations, wins and learning experiences brought out all my thoughts on paper! The leadership coursework had me create my Personal Success Plan, PSP, to consolidate these thoughts and I left the course feeling empowered, confident and ready to tackle the professional space — all thanks to Shelmina, Dona, Karuna and my amazing cohort!"

KG

Krishnapriya Gelli

Senior Software Engineer at USAA

"Participating in the Show Your Worth Circles was a transformative experience that allowed me to reflect deeply on myself and focus on intentional practices that have supported my career growth. The sessions were not only engaging and insightful but also provided a valuable opportunity to connect with like-minded women."

AS

Anara Satkeeva

Software Engineer at Snap

"Show Your Worth Circles came into my life at a moment when I knew I was capable of more but didn't fully trust myself to go after it yet. Being part of this community helped me rebuild confidence in a very real, grounded way. Hearing from others who were navigating similar challenges made me realize I wasn't alone, and that my doubts weren't a reflection of my abilities."

"The circles gave me a safe space to practice articulating my value, talk through career decisions, and step into leadership more intentionally. Over time, I became more confident speaking up, advocating for myself, and owning the impact of my work. That confidence translated directly into how I showed up in interviews, negotiations, and conversations about growth."

"Just as important as the skills was the community. Having consistent support, honest feedback, and encouragement from people who genuinely wanted to see me succeed made a huge difference. Show Your Worth Circles didn't just help me prepare for a higher-paying role — it helped me believe I deserved it and had the leadership skills to thrive in it."
