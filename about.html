Meet Shelmina Babai Abji

Founder & CEO, Speaker, Author, Board Member, Philanthropist

Shelmina's mission is to advance gender equality in leadership roles. She is the Founder and CEO of Show Your Worth.ai. She is the best-selling author of Show Your Worth: 8 Intentional Strategies for Women to Emerge as Leaders at Work, a renowned global keynote speaker, gender equality advocate, TEDx speaker, former IBM Vice President, angel investor, philanthropist, and a distinguished alumni.

She started with humble beginnings in Tanzania and went from being first in her family to graduate college to becoming one of the highest ranking women of color at IBM — while raising her two children as a single mother since they were 2 and 4. She started her career as a Software Engineer and moved to sales and sales leadership, leading global teams and delivering over $1B in revenues annually while consistently maintaining high client satisfaction and team morale.

Show Your Worth Circles was born from the desire to take these frameworks from the page into practice. By creating intimate, guided cohort experiences, Shelmina and her team provide the community, accountability, and specific coaching needed for real transformation.

Creator & Program Architect
