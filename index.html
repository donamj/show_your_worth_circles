Our mission

Build a success mindset. Own your Personal Success Plan.

Show Your Worth Circles is a transformative 10-week cohort experience rooted in Shelmina Abji's 8 practical, proven strategies for career advancement and personal fulfillment. It's a structured journey of deep reflection, meaningful conversations, and powerful mindset shifts.

Our mission is to equip women, from those just starting their careers to those aiming for the executive suite, with the personalized keys to define their success and execute their own Personal Success Plans (PSPs). This program provides a continuous support system, fostering an environment where women can truly build a success mindset within a vibrant, supportive community.

10 Weeks of guided cohort work
8 Intentional strategies, one per week
7–10 Women per circle, guided by a trained Cohort Lead

What sets SYW Circles apart

Built to keep working long after the cohort ends.

  • 01

    A Personal Success Plan that keeps growing

    Participants build a living Personal Success Plan that keeps evolving well after the cohort ends.

  • 02

    A like-minded community to think and grow with

    Brainstorm, problem-solve, and grow alongside a small group of women who understand exactly what you're navigating — translating insight into real application.

  • 03

    A private LinkedIn community

    Connects participants with women from other cohorts, not just their own — plus direct interaction with Shelmina herself, who is active in the group.

  • 04

    Structured workbooks

    Every session is anchored by a guided workbook tied to that week's strategy — concrete, repeatable practice, not a conversation that fades by Monday.

  • 05

    ShowYourWorth.ai support between sessions

    Participants use ShowYourWorth.ai to reflect, get feedback, and keep building their plan between sessions.

  • 06

    A resource that keeps working

    Every participant receives a companion e-book copy of the Show Your Worth book — a resource to return to long after the final session.

  • 07

    Small enough for real accountability

    Circles of 7–10, guided by a trained Cohort Lead, mean every participant is known, expected to show up, and supported in doing so.
