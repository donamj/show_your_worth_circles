Why join a circle

More than a leadership program — a guided transformation.

Immense leadership growth happens in community, where we inspire and learn from each other. Our carefully curated groups provide the framework, support, and accountability needed for sustainable advancement. Built on the proven strategies from Shelmina Abji, the program combines structured learning, meaningful peer discussions, and practical application to help you build a strong success mindset and gain the confidence to pursue bigger opportunities.

You'll develop a personalized Personal Success Plan (PSP) aligned with your goals, and receive ongoing accountability from a supportive community. If you're ready for lasting growth, clarity, confidence, and a community that will champion your success — Show Your Worth Circles is for you.

What you'll gain

Four things every circle is built to give you.

  • 01

    A strong success mindset, reinforced week after week

    Each session is intentionally designed to shift how you think, building momentum that compounds over the 10 sessions so the mindset becomes second nature.

  • 02

    A Personal Success Plan (PSP) — a living, working document tied to your real goals

    You leave with a tangible, personalized roadmap — not a generic template — built around your actual ambitions and career context.

  • 03

    A deeply supportive peer community that keeps you accountable

    You're surrounded by peers who challenge you, celebrate wins with you, and hold you to the commitments you make to yourself.

  • 04

    Exclusive access to a private LinkedIn group for continued engagement

    Interactive time with Shelmina Abji, plus a private LinkedIn group to stay connected, share wins, ask questions, and keep the momentum going long after the program ends — with the same trusted circle.

Session structure

8 themed workbooks. 10 guided sessions.

The program is structured around 8 themed workbooks based on Shelmina Abji's best-selling book Show Your Worth: 8 Intentional Strategies for Women to Emerge as Leaders at Work, each guiding you through deep reflection and practical action. Sessions are facilitated by trained hosts and designed for small groups to foster trust and accountability.
