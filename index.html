Our mission

Every woman deserves a personalized definition of success — and a plan to execute it.

Show Your Worth Circles is a transformative 10-week cohort experience rooted in Shelmina Abji's eight practical, proven strategies for career advancement and personal fulfillment. It's a structured journey of deep reflection, meaningful conversation, and powerful mindset shifts.

We equip women — from those just starting their careers to those aiming for the executive suite — with the personalized keys to define their own success and execute a Personal Success Plan built around it. What you leave with isn't a worksheet. It's a continuous support system: a vibrant, supportive community built to help you hold the success mindset you came here to build.

Inside a circle

What actually happens for ten weeks.

  • 01

    Weekly Circle Sessions

    Live, small-group conversation with women at every career stage — not a webinar, a room you're expected to speak in.

  • 02

    Guided Reflection

    Structured prompts between sessions that turn a week's insight into evidence you can act on, not a feeling you forget.

  • 03

    Your Personal Success Plan (PSP)

    A living document you build week over week — not a workbook that gets shelved the day the cohort ends.

  • 04

    Peer Accountability

    A rotating check-in partner inside your circle, keeping your momentum honest between sessions.

  • 05

    Continuous Community

    The circle doesn't close on week ten. Alumnae stay connected — and keep showing up for each other — long after.

Shelmina Abji's eight strategies

Eight strategies. One throughline, every week.

These eight, proven strategies for career advancement are the spine of every circle session — each one orbiting the same center: the worth you're here to define and act on.

Your
Worth
01

Claim Your Seat

Show up for the opportunity before you feel fully ready — and stop waiting for permission that isn't coming.

02

Build Currency, Not Just Credentials

Turn your results into visible, repeatable proof of value — the kind that travels ahead of you into a room.

03

Speak So Rooms Remember

Communicate with the clarity and presence that gets you heard the first time, not the third.

04

Grow Deliberate Relationships

Build the sponsors and allies who advocate for you in the rooms you're not yet in.

05

Take the Calculated Risk

Move before every condition is perfect — that's precisely where advancement lives.

06

Lead Like You're Already There

Practice the mindset and habits of the next role before you're handed its title.

07

Negotiate Your Worth

Ask directly and clearly for what you've earned — without over-explaining or apologizing for the ask.

Design Your Personal Success Plan
